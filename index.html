This site hosts public repositories associated with published research from Achim Klug's lab at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus.

Our work focuses on auditory neuroscience, with an emphasis on sound localization and auditory brainstem circuits. Here you’ll find data, code, and analysis tools linked to our publications.

Repositories

Medial Superior Olive Model

A spiking neuronal network model of medial superior olive circuitry for analyzing spatial hearing perception under various axon myelination patterns.

Citation: Ben-Zheng Li, Sio Hang Pun, Mang I Vai, Tim Lei, Achim Klug (2022).
Predicting the Influence of Axon Myelination on Sound Localization Precision Using a Spiking Neural Network Model of Auditory Brainstem. Front. Neurosci. 16:840983.
https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2022.840983

Auditory Brainstem Response Model

A computational model for synthesizing auditory brainstem responses (ABR) by simulating auditory brainstem neural circuitry with a spiking neuronal network model.

Citation: Li, Ben-Zheng, Shani Poleg, Matthew Ridenour, Daniel Tollin, Tim Lei, and Achim Klug (2024).
Computational model for synthesizing auditory brainstem responses to assess neuronal alterations in aging and autistic animal models. bioRxiv.
https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.08.04.606499

Dendritic Spine Super-Resolution Image Processing

Dendritic spine data analysis and visualization using SIM microscopy images.

Citation: Li, Ben-Zheng, Anna Sumera, Sam A. Booker, and Elizabeth A. McCullagh (2023).
Current Best Practices for Analysis of Dendritic Spine Morphology and Number in Neurodevelopmental Disorder Research. ACS Chemical Neuroscience 14(9): 1561–1572.
https://doi.org/10.1021/acschemneuro.3c00062
